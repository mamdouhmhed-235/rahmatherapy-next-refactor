1 · Getting in

One address, one password.

Bookmark this

Your admin lives at rahmatherapy.uk/admin/login. There is no link to it from your normal website — type it in once and save it. It's a staff door only; clients never sign in.

Finding your way around

The bar across the bottom — Dashboard, Bookings, Clients, Enquiries, Staff, More — never goes away.

2 · Your day, start to finish

Every booking travels the same path. Once you know it, the whole system makes sense.

Booking arrives→ You confirm it→ You give it to a therapist→ The visit happens→ Mark it done & paid
The step people forget

Giving the job to a therapist. Nothing forces you to do it, and the client never sees whether it's been done — they think it's all arranged. A confirmed visit with nobody assigned is the failure that actually hurts.

Your morning

Use the Today tab

The main bookings list starts with the furthest-away date, so today sits partway down. The Today tab is the one you want each morning.

Your evening

Back to the dashboard. Outstanding tells you who still owes money. Enquiries tells you who you promised to ring back. Needs your attention should be empty — if it isn't, that's tomorrow's list.

3 · Confirming a booking

A booking from your website arrives as pending. Confirming tells the client it's really happening.

The two little buttons on each booking card

The pin opens the address in Maps. The … gives you Send reminder, Mark paid and Cancel booking — the quickest way to nudge someone about tomorrow. On a narrow phone that menu opens half off the edge of the screen. It still works; it just looks wrong.

4 · Giving the job to a therapist

The step that matters most, and the easiest one to miss.

  1. Open the bookingYou can't do this from the list — the "Assign" bar on your dashboard takes you to a list, but you still have to open each booking.
  2. Scroll right to the bottomThe page is long — past guidance, dates, payment and notes. Participants tells you whether they need a male or female therapist. Assignment is just below it.
  3. Press "Assign therapist"A list slides up from the bottom.
  4. Tap a nameThat's the whole thing. It saves the moment you tap.
It happens the instant you tap

There is no "are you sure?", and emails go straight out to both the client and the therapist.

The box then stays open and changes to "Reassign this booking". That means it worked — don't tap again.

When nobody can be picked

The most common cause

"Bookings disabled." Every new staff member starts switched off. Until you turn on Can take bookings on their profile, they can't be given any work — and nothing else will tell you.

5 · Finishing a visit

Two taps, and skipping them is the commonest way your numbers go wrong.

  1. Mark completeThe visit happened.
  2. Mark paidIf money changed hands. Cash or card.
If you skip it

The visit still shows as owing money, and the client still counts as someone who's never been. Your end-of-day numbers quietly stop being true.

6 · Moving, cancelling, no-shows

Home visits change. Here's each one.

  • They want to move it. Open the booking → Move appointment. Pick a new day and time. Nothing else changes.
  • They cancel. Open the booking → Status & payment → Cancel booking.
  • Nobody answered the door. Don't mark it complete — record it as a no-show instead, so your numbers stay honest.

7 · Taking a booking over the phone

Someone rings up. Bookings → New booking, then four steps.

Two things that will stop you dead

You must tap one of the suggested addresses. Typing it out and carrying on leaves the form stuck.

The calendar doesn't appear until postcode, town, client gender and service are all filled in. It looks broken. It's waiting.

Before you press create

"Send confirmation email to client" is already ticked. If you're just typing up a booking you already agreed, untick it — or you'll email a real person.

The consent box next to it is not ticked, and the form won't submit until it is.

Repeat bookings

Greyed out here. To set up a repeating visit, start from the client's own page instead.

8 · Enquiries

Someone asking a question, not booking yet. This is your "ring them back" list.

9 · Clients

Everyone who's ever booked, and their history.

Mind the tick-boxes

The small boxes down the left select several clients so you can delete them together. They're easy to catch with your thumb while scrolling. If a bar appears at the bottom of the screen, you've ticked something — untick it.

10 · Adding someone to your team

Staff → Add staff member. This creates their account and their password in one go.

Two things, or it won't work

The password must be at least 12 characters, or it won't be accepted.

Their gender isn't paperwork — it decides which clients they can ever be sent to. Many clients ask for a same-gender therapist.

Then do this, or they can't work

New staff start with bookings switched off. Open their profile and turn on Can take bookings, or they'll never appear when you try to assign a visit.

The switches save instantly

Active account and Can take bookings take effect the moment you tap them. The Save profile button above is only for their name and details.

When someone leaves

Don't delete them — set their role to Inactive / Suspended. That switches off their access and keeps their history.

11 · When someone forgets their password

This one is entirely your job, and it has a time limit.

  1. They tap "Forgot your password?"Nothing is emailed to them yet. The request comes to you.
  2. You go to More → Password requestsEvery waiting request is listed there.
  3. You press ApproveThey're emailed a one-time link — and the same link is shown on your screen, so you can read it out or message it to them.
  4. They set a new passwordUsing that link.
Approve it the same day

The link stops working after 24 hours. After that they have to start again.

If YOU forget your own password

There's nobody above you to approve it. Keep your own sign-in details written down somewhere safe, and if you're ever locked out, contact whoever looks after the website for you.

12 · The four roles

Each role sees a different home screen. A therapist's app really is smaller — that's on purpose. Give people the smallest role that lets them do their job.

Owner

Give it to: yourself. One person.

  • Everything, including changing what other people are allowed to do.
  • The only role that can change permissions.

Admin / Practice Manager

Give it to: a trusted manager who runs things while you're treating.

  • Everything you can do — bookings, clients, staff, prices, money.
  • Can't change permissions.

Client Care / Booking Coordinator

Give it to: whoever answers the phone and the messages.

  • Enquiries, all bookings, all clients — confirm, move and cancel.
  • Can't change prices, services, opening hours or settings.
  • Can't add or edit staff — they see a read-only team list.
About money

Money is kept off their dashboard — but this isn't a lock. Someone who knows the web address can still reach the reports page. Give this role to someone you'd trust with the numbers anyway.

Therapist

Give it to: everyone who does visits.

  • Their own day, their own visits, and the address and phone number for each.
  • Mark a visit complete, or record a no-show. Add treatment notes.
  • Can't see the client list, or anyone else's bookings.
  • Can't move or cancel a visit — they'll ring you.
  • Can't see whether a visit has been paid for.

13 · Services and hours

The two settings you'll actually touch. Both live under More.

Check this number first

If Female says 0, every client who asks for a woman will be told there's no availability — no matter how empty your calendar looks. Same for Male. It's the fastest answer to "why can't anyone book?"

14 · Things that catch people out

Worth knowing before they confuse you.

1 · New staff can't take work

Every new person starts with bookings switched off. Turn on Can take bookings on their profile.

2 · Your own numbers will say zero — and that's right

The top block on your dashboard counts only visits assigned to you. If you don't do visits yourself, it stays at zero for ever. The row underneath is the whole clinic — that's the one you want. Check the Team / Mine switch is on Team.

3 · The "Assign" bar doesn't assign

The blue bar on your dashboard takes you to a list of bookings that need a therapist — but you still have to open each one to assign it. That's how it works.

4 · Assigning happens instantly

No confirmation, and two emails go out immediately. The box staying open and saying "Reassign" means it worked. See number 8.

5 · "Claimable" means something different for everyone

Next to Today and Upcoming, which show the whole clinic, Claimable shows only what the person looking can personally take — their gender, their switch. Two people will see different numbers.

6 · "Next visit: nothing scheduled"

You may see this above a day that clearly has visits. It means nothing is scheduled later today — the ones already past don't count.

7 · Numbers look wrong for a second

Occasionally the dashboard shows zeros right after signing in. Pull to refresh. It's a loading hiccup, not lost data.

8 · Nothing gets deleted without asking you

Every change is recorded with who did it and when, and deletions always confirm first. Some things do happen instantly though — assigning a therapist, and the two switches on a staff profile.
