1 · Getting in

One page, one password. Everything else lives behind it.

Worth knowing

The bar across the bottom — Dashboard, Bookings, Clients, Enquiries, Staff, More — is how you move around. It's always there.

2 · Your day, start to finish

Every booking travels the same path. Once you know it, the whole system makes sense.

Booking arrives→ You confirm it→ You give it to a therapist→ The visit happens→ Mark it done & paid
The step people forget

Giving the job to a therapist. Nothing forces you to do it, and the client never sees whether it's been done — they think it's all booked. A confirmed visit with nobody assigned is the failure that actually hurts.

Your morning

Your evening

Back to the dashboard. Outstanding tells you who still owes money. Enquiries tells you who you promised to ring back. Needs your attention should be empty — if it isn't, that's tomorrow's list.

3 · Confirming a booking

A booking from your website arrives as pending. Confirming is what tells the client it's really happening.

4 · Giving the job to a therapist

The step that matters most, and the easiest one to miss.

  1. Open the bookingYou can't do this from the list — you have to open each booking.
  2. Find the assignment panelIt shows who's needed and whether anyone's been picked.
  3. Tap a therapist's nameThat's it — it saves straight away.
It saves instantly

There's no "are you sure?". The moment you tap a name it's assigned and two emails go out. The box also stays open and changes to "Reassign this booking" — that's normal, not a mistake. It worked.

When nobody can be picked

The most common cause

"Bookings disabled." Every new staff member starts switched off. Until you turn on Can take bookings on their profile, they cannot be given any work — and nothing else will tell you.

5 · Finishing a visit

Two taps, and skipping them is the commonest way your numbers go wrong.

  1. Mark completeThe visit happened.
  2. Mark paidIf money changed hands. Cash or card.
If you skip it

The visit still shows as owing money, and the client still counts as someone who's never been. Your end-of-day numbers quietly stop being true.

9 · Adding someone to your team

Staff → Add staff member. This creates their account and their password in one go.

Do this next, or they can't work

New staff start with bookings switched off. Open their profile and turn on Can take bookings, or they'll never appear when you try to assign a visit.

When someone leaves

Don't delete them — set their role to Inactive / Suspended. That switches off their access but keeps their history intact.

10 · The four roles

These aren't the same screen with buttons hidden. Each role is genuinely a different app — give people the smallest one that lets them do their job.

Owner

Give it to: yourself. One person.

Everything, including handing out permissions. The only role that can.

Admin / Practice Manager

Give it to: a trusted manager who runs things while you're treating.

Everything you can do, except changing permissions.

Client Care / Booking Coordinator

Give it to: whoever answers the phone and the messages.

The safest role that still gets real work done. They handle enquiries, bookings and coverage — but no money on their dashboard, no prices, no settings.

Therapist

Give it to: everyone who does visits.

The smallest, safest role. They see their own day and nothing else — no client list, no money, no settings.

What they can't do

They can't move or cancel a visit, and they can't see whether it's been paid. If a client asks to reschedule at the door, they'll ring you.

12 · Things that catch people out

The handful of things worth knowing before they confuse you.

1 · New staff can't take work

Every new person starts with bookings switched off. Turn on Can take bookings on their profile, or they'll never appear when you assign.

2 · "Assign 3 unassigned" doesn't assign

That button takes you to a list, but you can't assign from the list — you have to open each booking. Slightly annoying, entirely normal.

3 · Assigning happens instantly

No confirmation, and two emails go out immediately. The box staying open and saying "Reassign" means it worked.

4 · "Next visit: nothing scheduled"

You may see this above a day that clearly has visits. It means nothing is scheduled later today — the ones already past don't count.

5 · Numbers look wrong for a second

Occasionally the dashboard shows zeros right after signing in. Pull to refresh. It's a loading hiccup, not lost data.

6 · You can't really break it

Every change is recorded with who did it and when. Nothing you tap deletes a client or a booking without asking you first.
